virus 94

Are you ready to escape your monotonous reality and cling to the last shred of survival? Welcome to Virus 94—the psychological horror experience set to redefine survival gaming. Here, mercy does not exist, and the thin line between humanity and monstrosity has completely vanished. As the disease ravages everything in its path, the fate of your kind hangs in your hands. But survival comes with a price... Are you ready to sacrifice everything for them, or will your survival instinct drive you to betray them? Remember, as you walk through this darkness: the real danger doesn't lurk in the monsters hunting you, but in the mirrors... For your first and ultimate enemy is yourself!
